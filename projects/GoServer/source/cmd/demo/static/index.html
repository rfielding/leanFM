    Rules as implemented

    The board is bounded by a Wall. After a stone is placed, every adjacent group of another colour with no liberty is removed and counted as its owner's losses (the bowl shows who captured it). If nothing was captured and the placed group has no liberty, it is removed as suicide and counted as the mover's losses; suicide is legal. Territory is an empty region bordered only by one colour's stones and the Wall. Score = Territory − Losses; a team's score is its members' sum.

    Teams default to one per seat; the creator may group seats before the start, and during play two teams merge permanently when every person on both accepts. The game ends when every remaining player has passed since the last stone, after a full round of timeouts, or after 3·N² moves. An unanswered turn is a timeout. With the ko rule on, simple ko plus situational superko apply; Auto turns it on only for two players. Bots use maxn Monte Carlo tree search on the server; their search for each move is shown once it is played.
